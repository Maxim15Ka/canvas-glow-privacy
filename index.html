Canvas Glow privacy & data

Updated November 11, 2026 · version 3.2.0

Canvas Glow personalizes Canvas LMS and adds study tools inside your browser. It has no developer-operated server, analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, or Canvas Glow account. The extension does not send your courses, grades, notes, drafts, or images to its developer.

What the extension uses

  • Your signed-in Canvas site supplies course names and IDs, current grades, assignments, quizzes, announcements, discussion descriptions, calendar and planner information needed for the features you enable. Requests go to that same Canvas site's API using the login already managed by your browser. The extension does not ask for your password or API token.
  • Visual and feature preferences, including your chosen theme, schedule and accessibility settings, use chrome.storage.sync. Your browser provider may synchronize these preferences across devices when browser sync is enabled. Academic and navigation preferences are stored locally by school/account rather than synchronized.
  • Uploaded images, saved themes, favorites, course preferences, grading rubrics, credits and goals, prior GPA/credits, navigation configuration, discovered course/menu information, grade history, planner completion, sticky notes, writing drafts and streak information use chrome.storage.local in this browser profile. The extension does not synchronize these through browser sync.
  • Draft saving reads editable text on supported Canvas pages to recover unfinished writing. Sticky notes and drafts are associated with page paths. Drafts older than 14 days are not offered for recovery; they can remain in local storage until you delete them. Grade history keeps up to 120 daily entries per course.

School and account separation

Canvas Glow verifies the signed-in user through the same Canvas site's read-only /api/v1/users/self/profile endpoint. It uses the Canvas user ID and display name from this response and stores those with the Canvas site origin and a last-seen timestamp locally. Other profile fields are not saved. Academic data and navigation use a separate storage namespace for each exact site origin and user ID, so matching course IDs and page paths on different accounts do not share notes, grades or rubrics.

Theme collections, uploaded custom-theme images, favorites and visual preferences are shared across Canvas profiles in this browser. Per-course images belong to their school/account. Settings identifies which profile you are editing. If account verification fails or a changed login is detected, academic tools pause and you must refresh Canvas before they resume.

Previous versions did not identify the owner of local academic data. Upgrade preserves this older data separately and moves previous academic/navigation sync settings into local recovery storage. You can review and explicitly assign the old data to one verified profile. It is not automatically attached to the next login. Original entries remain in full backups for recovery.

Images, speech and files

Default illustrated collections are bundled with the extension and work without an image server. Uploaded pictures are decoded, cropped and compressed on your device. If you use an external URL for a course image, loading it contacts that image host, which receives normal request information such as your IP address. Prefer an uploaded image for a local copy.

Read-aloud uses your browser's speech synthesis service and selected system voice. Voice processing may depend on your browser or operating system. Canvas Glow runs no speech service of its own.

Theme exports include the selected theme's name, palette and images. Full backups include all saved school/account profiles, preferences and locally saved extension data, which may contain private schoolwork, notes and grades. Export only to a location you trust and share theme exports when you only want to share a look. Calendar exports include assignment and due-date details.

Your controls

Disable individual features in Settings, or turn Canvas Glow off from the popup. Turning a feature off stops its active behavior but does not erase existing data. Under Settings → Data, export a full backup, delete notes and drafts for the selected profile, or reset browser-wide visual preferences and that profile's academic/navigation settings. Other profiles' academic data stays separate. To remove all extension storage, remove the extension from this browser profile; synchronized preferences may also be managed by your browser's sync controls.

Data is stored in the browser profile, not encrypted separately by Canvas Glow. People with access to that profile may be able to access it. The school/account namespaces prevent accidental mixing within the extension; they are not an access-control boundary against someone who has access to the same browser profile or an exported backup. Use separate browser profiles for different people.

Use and sharing

Canvas Glow uses Canvas information only to provide its visible customization and study features. The developer does not collect or sell this information, use it for advertising, transfer it to data brokers, or use it to train models. This version has no payments or third-party integrations.

Independent project

Canvas Glow is an independent browser extension and is not affiliated with Instructure, Canvas, or your school. GPA and grade simulations are estimates; your school's grading policies and Canvas gradebook remain authoritative.
